# Partial ETF grid fill: accounting and ticket reconciliation

Reusable procedure, not a broker execution protocol. User confirmation must identify code, side, filled quantity, actual execution price; a multi-action ticket cannot be applied wholesale.

1. Read current ledger, active ticket, confirmed fill history, and trading date. Assert exactly one matching action and no earlier booking of the same fill; validate T+1 sellable pool, amount, and grid target. Do not infer fills from a market high/low or a pending order.
2. For a fully sold *eligible* target grid, reduce that target lot's ledger quantity, portfolio quantity and cost basis (including its allocated purchase fee). Add `quantity × executed price − provisional sell fee` to cash. Record the one filled action in history, with fee assumption and net proceeds. If target is not eligible and old shares funded the sale, an independent settled-inventory accounting is necessary; do not reuse this simplified target-lot retirement.
3. Archive the pre-fill ticket and remove only its filled action. Preserve unrelated pending recommendations, especially already-resting lower buy limits. Add a new rebuy limit at `round(actual sell price / (1 + gap), 3)` and, if available inventory supports it, the next lowest target sell as **new suggestions**. Distinguish new suggestions from confirmed live broker orders.
4. Check all sell suggestions together against T+1 pool (including other resting sells), all potential buys against cash floor and per-ETF cap. On a ticket refresh, validate a rebuy against its **actual fill-derived** target and a distinct staged lower buy against `round(rebuy_price / (1 + gap), 3)`; do not compare it to the older surviving lot's theoretical next grid. On a new-day ticket, generate rebuy **plus** the nearest eligible sell **plus** the lower staged buy, reserving rebuy cash and cap first; give the rebuy its own ID (`D-rebuy`) rather than reusing the next staged ID (`E`). Regression-test both pathways against isolated `LEDGER` and `TICKETS` paths, then compare the real ledger checksum before/after tests. For any replacement sell, changing a local ticket is not a broker cancellation: require explicit reconciliation of old resting orders.
5. Read back the exact ledger and active ticket: the fill appears once in history, old sell action is gone, other actions did not become fills, quantity/cash/cost basis reconcile, and the new action list renders with grid letters. Broker fee/commission remains provisional until the 银河交割单 is known.

Illustrative 2026-09-29 case: user confirmed 159992 C sell 9,400 @ 0.863 (C originally bought 9,400 @ 0.846 and was T+1-eligible). At provisional ¥5 each-side fees, net proceeds ¥8,107.20, removed cost ¥7,957.40, provisional round-trip profit ¥149.80, rebuy 9,400 @ 0.846.

Second regression fixture (2026-09-30): 159363 D bought 5,100 @ 1.169, sold 5,100 @ 1.192; with provisional ¥5 each side, sell net ¥6,074.20, removed cost ¥5,966.90, round-trip profit ¥107.30. The rebuy is 5,100 @ 1.169; staged E remains 5,200 @ 1.146; surviving C can suggest 4,800 @ 1.221 if the sellable pool and participation core permit. The lower staged price follows the actual-sale-derived rebuy (1.169/1.02), **not** theoretical C-bottom 1.197/1.02. The local C sell and D rebuy are suggestions until the user confirms broker placement; unrelated pending actions are not fills. These historical data are test fixtures, **not** live order instructions.
