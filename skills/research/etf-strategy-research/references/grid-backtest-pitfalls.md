# 159612: why daily-range extrapolation was wrong

Case: an ETF screener's `grid_tool.py` reported `cross30` as the number of days for which `(high-low)/low` exceeded **best_step_pct**, an ADR-derived threshold (floor 0.6%), which could differ from the 1% strategy gap. A temporary `debug_roi3.py` fixed `CROSS_DAYS=23`, multiplied by a conditional per-round spread and annualized to ~52%. This figure was retracted: neither sequence (low before high), executable trigger, broker confirmation nor completed buy/sell follows from a daily high/low pair.

The repo's chronological `tools/etf_research/grid_model.py` uses previous five-minute close as signal and next-open with adverse slippage as execution approximation, plus cash/inventory/fee/T+1 checks. Its `engine.py` at review time called `simulate` with 2% and different 5,000-yuan normalized slots; do not relabel those results as a 1%/8,000-yuan strategy. A separate `tools/etf_research/plan_a_159612.py` driver was exercised for research with 2.066–2.160 bounds, 2.105 anchor, 1% gap, 8,000 per lot, 40,000 simulated cap, empty starting inventory. For 2026-09-01 through 10-13 (25 sessions, 1,200 five-minute bars) it produced two simulated round trips, 163.20 yuan realized net, 0 end inventory and only 7,878.40 yuan peak deployed. Tests passed (12 including neighboring research tests). This demonstrates a reproducible sequential calculation, **not** expected annual yield: parameters selected 10-13 were applied retroactively; fills and anchor reset are approximations, not validated against actual 同花顺 semi-automatic execution; one-lot deployment cannot stress full-capital or box-break risks. Treat exact numbers as dated session examples, not reusable market facts.

The user's screenshot at the time showed semi-auto reminder, 8,000 yuan each action, and an initial price interval 1.000–4.000 that differs from research bounds. Never infer order placement, completed fills, max inventory cap or initial shares from a confirmation screenshot alone. Check actual fields and live broker state for execution.
