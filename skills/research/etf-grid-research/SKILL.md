# ETF grid research

1. Establish budget, drawdown tolerance, access, fees, and horizon. Long-hold suitability and high volatility do not establish grid suitability. Load grounded-citations.
2. Save current quotes, dated official macro/fund/index disclosures and historical bars. Verified Tencent endpoints: `https://qt.gtimg.cn/q=sh512100,...` and `https://web.ifzq.gtimg.cn/appstock/app/fqkline/get?param=sh512100,day,,,640,qfq`. Inspect qfqday/day; rows date/open/close/high/low/volume. Exclude incomplete current candle; verify units, chronology, OHLC and counts. HK quotes may be delayed.
3. Cross-check recent closes. AKShare fund_etf_hist_sina uses MiniRacer; run sequentially on Windows, as parallel decoder initialization caused fatal partition_address_space assertion. Sina prices are unadjusted: don't mix with qfq across distributions.
4. Compare returns, MA20/60, range position, realized volatility and threshold reversals. Reversals are not completed profitable cycles.
5. Standardize budget, initial inventory, core, cash, fees/minimum, slippage, rounding, signals and execution; test multiple spacings and rolling starts without asset-specific cherry-picking. User confirmed 万一免五 on 2026-09-30 for the current two A-share grid ETFs: use 0.0001 per side with no RMB5 minimum for new research, labeling broker rounding assumptions. Old runs may still carry min5; do not reuse them as no-minimum results. An isolated fee-only re-run is saved at `cache/grid-gap-discussion-20260930/recalculate.py`: reproduces old runs first, overrides only the imported model's fee in memory, and checks live script/ledger/ticket hashes unchanged. Production `grid_account.fee` still had min5 at this check; historical ledger fees need broker reconciliation, not automatic backfill. With daily bars use previous-close signals/next-open fills; never assume intraday OHLC order. This model isn't live resting limit orders or necessarily a lower-bound return.
6. Equity must include inventory losses and cash. Distinguish initial inventory sales from rebuy/sell cycles. Compare same initial exposure and maximum capital buy-and-hold. Show losing periods. Overlapping windows aren't independent success probabilities.
7. Test flat-path costs, no same-day execution, adverse opening gaps, downtrend inventory trapping, cycle counting, budget/lot constraints and dates.
8. Rank current regime separately from historical returns. Give horizon, conditional entry, invalidation, risk cap, stress losses, and confidence. Proposed stops or trial sizing absent from simulation are untested overlays. Preserve portfolio drawdown caveats.
9. Deliver report and reproducible data/code archive. Don't claim daily bars capture T+0 advantages, real fills, intraday worst drawdowns, precise dividend reinvestment or guaranteed loss limits. Don't modify production recommendation logic for research.
10. Do not recommend from a hand-picked handful of ETFs. Start from a listed universe (Sina ETF list was 1676 on 2026-09-23), publish coverage, liquidity, history, and exclusion counts. Point-in-time turnover screens are biased; say so.
11. Minute verification: Sina `money.finance.sina.com.cn/.../CN_MarketData.getKLineData` scale=5 datalen=10000 returned ~5001 bars (~Apr-Sep 2026); Tencent mkline returned 320 regardless of requested length. Sina scale=1 returned null. Tencent vs Sina overlapping 5m closes can differ by ticks; 15:00 often matches. Do not treat two Sina hosts as independent vendors. Volume units differ (~100x) and still disagree bar-by-bar.
12. Engine: previous 5m close signal, next-open IOC with slippage/tick, T+1 settled shares, lot size 100, min commission, inventory cost cap. Do not fill on high/low. Disclose if participation uses the same bar's total volume (lookahead). Count completed rebuy/sell cycles separately from initial-inventory sales and mark-to-market.
13. T+0 is a legal category, not a name: SSE/SZSE 2026 rule 3.1.5 covers bond ETF, money-market ETF, gold ETF, commodity-futures ETF, and cross-border ETF/LOF only if the tracked underlyings themselves allow same-day turnaround. Gold-stock ETFs and generic QDII names are not T+0 by default. HKEX ETFs can be bought and sold same day, settle T+2, are stamp-duty exempt, and board lots are not 100; do not apply A-share commission to HK until the broker confirms. HK minute history was not available from the A-share Tencent mkline endpoint.
14. Deduplicate by return correlation / shared index before treating products as diversification. Prefer the more liquid share class when tracking error and fees are comparable.
15. For a third ETF, fix the user's intended post-migration baseline first (2026-09-30 example: 589720 + 159363), then rerun every eligible historical candidate rather than selecting old top-10 names. Compare complete-cycle net / time-average inventory cost, weaker half-window efficiency, recent-window efficiency, ordinary/down-day correlations, and complete-cycle sales/net on dates when BOTH baseline ETFs have no sale. Disclose that this uses separate normalized accounts, not a jointly funded shared-account backtest; never add independent-account profits into the user's account forecast. Preserve failed initializations and missing scores, assert counts, separate current daily correlations/quotes from older minute simulation dates, and test fee and fill assumptions in isolated in-memory models. If the final research preference is not the raw weighted-score leader, show the actual leader, score gap, preference trade-off and weight sensitivity. Research example `cache/grid-third-20260930/` scans all 321 eligible historical funds (not all currently listed ETFs), with modern-fee re-runs, all-event idle-cycle attribution, updated unadjusted daily correlations, and source PDFs. A new ETF recommendation is not funding or buy authorization. Require actual shared-capital allocation simulation and broker/order reconciliation before activation.
